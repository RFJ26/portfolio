## The challenge

The restaurant only had a Facebook page and the menu was an outdated photo. Customers kept calling to ask about opening hours and table availability.

## The solution

- Menu stored in content files the owner can update without touching code.
- Opening hours, map and a call button visible straight away on mobile.
- Booking request form delivered by email.

## Result

Fewer repetitive calls and more bookings made outside opening hours.
