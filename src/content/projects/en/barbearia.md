## The challenge

The barbershop wanted to spend less time on the phone and show off the barbers' work.

## The solution

A lightweight website in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no dependencies, featuring the price list, a gallery and a booking button that is always visible on mobile.

## Result

It loads in under a second on 4G, and most bookings are now made online.
