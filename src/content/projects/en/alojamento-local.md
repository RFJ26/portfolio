## The challenge

The rental depended on booking platforms and paid high commissions. It wanted a direct channel for returning guests.

## The solution

- Website in Portuguese, English and German.
- Room gallery with automatically optimised images.
- Availability calendar synced via iCal.
- Direct booking request form.

## Result

Some of the bookings from returning guests are now made directly.
