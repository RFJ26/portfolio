## The challenge

The bookshop received lots of messages asking whether a particular book was in stock.

## The solution

- Catalogue with search by title, author and genre.
- Admin panel to add books and mark availability.
- Button to reserve a book and collect it in store.

## What I learned

Data modelling in MySQL, server-side form validation and basic protection against SQL injection with prepared statements.
